Quick Decision Guide

V2 App Model vs V3 License Model

Clear criteria to help partners and sales reps recommend the right architecture for each client.

V2 — App Model

  • Pricing Pay per application monthly subscription.
  • Target Customer Small & medium businesses (SMBs), single-branch offices, and startups.
  • Architecture Modular — buy only the specific apps you need (e.g., BizPhone, Wallboard, Fax).
  • Best For Low initial cost, self-service setup in minutes, and standalone phone system needs.

V3 — License Model

  • Pricing Pay per user license monthly or annual agreement.
  • Target Customer Mid-market, enterprises, contact centers, and multi-location organizations.
  • Architecture Unified suite — Phone System, Contact Center, and MS Teams calling in one platform.
  • Best For Comprehensive enterprise communications, omni-channel ACD, and MS Teams integration.
